💡 Farming Tips & Best Practices

Expert advice to help you grow healthier crops and increase yields

💧 Smart Irrigation

Water early in the morning or late evening to reduce evaporation. Use drip irrigation for vegetables — it saves up to 50% water compared to flood irrigation. Monitor soil moisture before watering.

🌱 Soil Health

Test your soil every 2–3 years. Add organic compost (5–10 tons/hectare) before each season. Rotate crops to prevent nutrient depletion and reduce pest buildup in the soil.

🐛 Pest Management

Use Integrated Pest Management (IPM): start with cultural methods, then biological controls like neem oil, and chemical pesticides only as a last resort. Inspect crops weekly.

🧪 Fertilizer Use

Apply fertilizers based on soil test results. Split nitrogen application into 2–3 doses for better uptake. Combine chemical fertilizers with organic manure for balanced nutrition.

📅 Season Planning

Plan your crop calendar based on local rainfall patterns. Kharif crops (June–Oct) need monsoon rains. Rabi crops (Nov–Apr) rely on irrigation. Keep 2-week buffer for sowing.

♻️ Organic Farming

Use vermicompost, green manure, and bio-pesticides for chemical-free produce. Organic crops often fetch 20–30% higher prices in the market. Certification adds further value.

🌤️ Weather Awareness

Check weather forecasts before spraying pesticides or harvesting. Avoid field work during heavy rain. Protect young seedlings from extreme heat with shade nets.

📦 Post-Harvest Care

Harvest at the right maturity stage. Dry grains to 12–14% moisture before storage. Use clean, ventilated storage to prevent fungal damage and pest infestation.
